# Governance

Raion is owned and maintained by a single maintainer.

**The maintainer decides:**

- the product: features, user experience, documentation and roadmap
- the architecture, design and technology choices
- what is accepted, deferred or declined, among issues and pull requests
- releases, and the project's policies, including this one

**Everyone else** takes part as a user of the project: by reporting bugs, usability and documentation problems, and by making suggestions (see [CONTRIBUTING](CONTRIBUTING.md)). Pull requests are accepted only within the scope the maintainer agrees in an issue. Contributing does not confer a role in the project's design or direction.

**Open source.** The code is licensed under [Apache-2.0](LICENSE): you may use, change and redistribute it under that licence, including in your own fork. This governance applies to this project only.
